Admissions

Join the KJA family

Our admissions process is designed to be simple and personal — from your first inquiry to your child's first day.

How It Works

Four steps to enrollment

1

Inquire

Tell us about your child using the form below, or call our admissions office.

2

Tour & assessment

Visit campus and your child completes a short, age-appropriate placement assessment.

3

Submit documents

Share past school reports, a birth certificate, and passport photos.

4

Offer & enrollment

Receive your offer letter and secure your child's place with the enrollment deposit.

Requirements

What you'll need, by level

  • Copy of birth certificate
  • Up-to-date immunization record
  • Two recent passport photos
  • Non-refundable registration fee
  • Previous school report (last two terms)
  • Transfer letter, if changing schools
  • Copy of birth certificate
  • Placement assessment in English and Mathematics
  • Previous two years of school reports
  • Recommendation letter from current school
  • UNEB results, for entry into Senior 5
  • Placement assessment in English and Mathematics
Fees

Tuition & fees

Tuition varies by grade level and includes tuition, activity, and materials fees. A detailed schedule is shared with every applicant family.

About KJA

Our story

Founded to give Kampala's children an education that is rigorous, caring, and rooted in community.

Since 2005

Two decades of growing with our students

Kampala Junior Academy opened its doors with a single classroom and a simple belief: every child deserves teachers who know their name and a curriculum that stretches them. Two decades on, that belief still shapes everything we do — from our class sizes to our staffroom culture.

"We're not just preparing students for exams. We're preparing them for life."
— Head Teacher, Kampala Junior Academy
